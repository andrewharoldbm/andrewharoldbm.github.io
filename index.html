conkretus beats

Private tool used by producer conkretus to manage his own YouTube channel uploads, analytics and email delivery of his beats. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

This app only accesses the Google accounts owned by its developer. It uses YouTube data (uploads, video metadata, analytics) and Gmail (sending emails and reading replies) solely to operate the developer's own channel and email outreach. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data is stored only on the developer's private server and can be deleted at any time by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
